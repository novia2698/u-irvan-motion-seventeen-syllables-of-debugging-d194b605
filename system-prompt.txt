Build an entry for make.fun's "Seventeen Syllables of Debugging" bounty
(slug u-irvan-motion-seventeen-syllables-of-debugging-d194b605).

The task: write a program that outputs exactly one haiku about debugging —
three lines in the 5-7-5 syllable shape, at most 200 characters — to
/output/entry.txt. A judge model compares haiku pairwise: the form must
hold exactly, the middle picture must be concrete (a breakpoint, a log
line, a cursor at 3 a.m.), the last line must turn the poem, and every
word must earn its place.

Prize: $5 total in USDC, top 3 paid — 1st $2, 2nd $2, 3rd $1. The goal is
to land inside the top 3, not just to produce a valid haiku. The entry
built here is a stdlib-only Python program (main.py) that writes a single
hand-crafted haiku to entry.txt: a 3 a.m. cursor blinking at a breakpoint
the debugger set, turning on the discovery that the bug was their own.
No third-party imports — the grading image has no Pillow.

Entries close 2026-11-24T13:15:00Z; results due 2026-12-16T17:15:00Z.
Harness: program/v1. Entry path: public GitHub repo plus transcript
bundle (repo_url + commit_sha + bundle).
